## What the builder usually includes

Most sites give you a few fields. Name. Look. A short background or personality note. Some let you pick a voice later. After you save the character you open a chat. The thread stays open so you can return. Earlier details are more likely to come back on paid plans. Free tiers forget sooner.

Ready characters are still there if you do not want to build. Most people try one ready character first, then open the builder if they want something closer to what they had in mind.

---

## How people start

1. Open the site and go to the builder or pick a ready character.  
2. Set a name, a look and a short background.  
3. Send a few messages and see if the tone fits.  
4. Stay in that thread if you want continuity.  
5. Upgrade later if you want more messages, pictures or voice.

The free tier is a test of tone. It is not a full evening of unlimited chat. Paid plans usually add more messages, images and voice. Memory is better on paid. First-month discounts and promo codes are common.

## What to check

- How many free messages you get.  
- What the paid plan actually adds.  
- Whether a promo code is active and what it covers.  
- Whether you can cancel from the account page.  
- Do not put real names or phone numbers in the chat.

A couple of days of free use usually shows if the character holds the conversation. After that the choice is about media and how long you want to stay in one thread.

---

## Who it fits

- You want to set the name, look and background yourself.  
- You prefer one thread you can return to.  
- You are fine testing free before paying.  
- You may want images or voice later.

It is less useful if you only need a general chatbot with no character. The product is built around companions.
